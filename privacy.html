Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 October 2026

Hearth and Coin is a small shop in New Zealand. We sell printable planners, budget spreadsheets and wall art as digital downloads on Etsy at etsy.com/shop/HearthAndCoin. This policy covers this website and the Hearth and Coin pin tool, the small app we use with the Pinterest API.

What the pin tool does

The Hearth and Coin pin tool is a private, single-user app. It is used only by the shop owner, for one Pinterest business account: our own, @HearthAndCoin. No one else can sign in to it, and it is not offered to the public.

It does three things:

  • Publishes pins of our own products. It creates pins on our own boards, using our own product photos, and each pin links back to the matching listing in our Etsy shop. The shop owner reviews and approves every pin before it is scheduled to post.
  • Finds or creates our own boards, so each pin goes on the right board.
  • Reads statistics for our own pins (impressions, saves and clicks), so we can see which pins people find useful.

Information the pin tool uses

The tool connects to Pinterest through Pinterest's official OAuth sign-in. It asks only for the permissions it needs: to read our account details, to read and create our boards, and to read and create our pins. It never asks for or stores a Pinterest password.

It keeps only the following:

  • Access tokens. These are the sign-in tokens Pinterest gives us for our own account. They are stored encrypted, in private storage that only the shop owner can reach, and they are never shared.
  • A record of our own pins. For each of our pins: its ID, its board, the product it shows, when it was posted, and its impressions, saves and clicks. This lets us avoid posting the same pin twice and see what works.

The pin tool does not:

  • access, collect or store any other Pinterest user's information, such as profiles, boards, pins, followers or messages
  • follow, comment on, message or save anyone else's content
  • sell, rent or share any information with anyone
  • use Pinterest information for advertising elsewhere, or to train artificial intelligence or machine-learning models

Shoppers and this website

When you buy from us, the order is handled by Etsy, and Etsy's privacy policy applies. We only use the details Etsy shares with us to deliver your order and answer your messages. We never add them to a mailing list or share them.

This website has no sign-up, no forms, no cookies and no analytics. It is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Keeping information safe and deleting it

Tokens are encrypted, and our records are kept in private storage that only the shop owner can reach. Our own pin records are kept while the shop is running. To disconnect the pin tool, we revoke its access in Pinterest's settings under Security and logins, then Apps. That stops all access straight away, and we then delete the stored tokens.

Your rights

We follow New Zealand's Privacy Act 2020. If you think we hold information about you, you can ask to see it, correct it or have it deleted. In practice the pin tool holds no information about anyone except our own business account.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. For any question about privacy, contact us through our Etsy shop, using the "Contact shop owner" button.
